Calculation rules

Base weights: AI 6, IM 12, DV 8, CS 20, Programming 20, Character 24. CT base 44; DDDO base 46. AI combines quiz (CT) and sketchbook (DDDO). Overall gives CT and DDDO equal weight.

CS bonus up to 2; Programming bonus up to 2 (higher eligible result from Guess or Alternate). Bonus adds to the numerator only. False and Exempt are excluded; blank and Missing count as zero. Missing Count in Grade columns default to eligible. AI Ethics and Personal Choice are always excluded. All percentages capped at 100%; zero denominator gives N/A. Unknown scores or missing required assignment columns stop calculation.

Student data stays in your browser. No network requests or external libraries.
